# 1. Rolling evaluation instead of a competitive call

Status: accepted

## Context

Many calls for projects collect everything until the deadline, score it and fund from the top down. That needs scoring criteria, a ranking step and bulk decisions.

## Decision

Applications are evaluated one by one as they come in. Good projects are funded until the call's budget runs out.

## Consequences

Rolling evaluation is a legitimate model, often used for smaller grants, and keeps the scope focused on the workflow: there are no scoring criteria, no ranking and no bulk decisions. Every approval takes its grant from what's left of the call's budget, which [ADR 14](0014-the-budget-cannot-be-overspent.md) protects against two approvals at once.
